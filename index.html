Archit RamanaSai Kambhamettu

I am a first-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Maryland (UMD), advised by Professor Abhinav Shrivastava and Professor Leo Liu.

My research interests are at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction and Computer Vision, primarily with developing novel approaches for domain-specific reasoning to assist field experts. I am particularly interested in applying this line of research to sports analytics and biomechanics.

I completed my B.S. in Computer Science with a double major in Mathematics and a notation in Sports Management at UMD College Park. Feel free to reach out to me for research collaborations! My email is architk@umd.edu.
