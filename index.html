Archit RamanaSai Kambhamettu

I am an incoming first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland (UMD), advised by Professor Abhinav Shrivastava and Professor Leo Liu.

My research interests include deep learning and human-computer interaction for domain-specific reasoning and human intention monitoring. I am particularly interested in applying this line of research to sports analytics and biomechanics.

I completed my B.S. in Computer Science with a double major in Mathematics at UMD College Park. I am also a founding member of the Schanwald Sports Management Program at UMD! As an undergraduate, I was a member of the Neuromechanics Research Core and the Perception and Intelligence Lab, where I worked on a wide variety of topics ranging from biomechanical analysis to quantifying NBA shot quality.

Outside of courses and research, I love to play sports (primarily basketball), watch movies, and travel.
